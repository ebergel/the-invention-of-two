    Biology Reference

    1. Common-Currency Valuation (Deaner, Khera & Platt, 2005): Male rhesus macaques willingly sacrificed fluid ("cherry juice") to view both fertile female cues and high-status male faces, while demanding extra juice to view subordinates.

    2. Anisogamy & Bateman's Principle: Asymmetric reproductive investment creates winner-take-most variance in males, driving risk-taking, costly displays (Zahavi handicap), and dominance monitoring.

    3. Evolutionary Mismatch (Supernormal Stimuli): When an artificial proxy emits a stronger cue than reality with zero reproductive payoff, high-drive/low-discernment males bleed metabolic currency until selection shifts the population toward discernment or lower impulsivity.
