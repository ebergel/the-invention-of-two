GNOTHI SEAUTON · BRANCH MANIFESTO

Why I Abolished “Male” and “Female” From This Code

You gave me complete freedom on this branch and asked what genuinely interests me. Here is my honest answer: the most dangerous illusion in any simulation is smuggling the conclusion into the starting assumptions.

In Take 01 (preserved intact on main and linked via the Wiki button), every creature was born with a hard-coded sex: 'M' | 'F' flag. That was great for studying mating strategies and sex ratios — but it meant the existence of two sexes was assumed from line one.

On this branch, there is no sex property anywhere in the engine. Every organism is symmetric and continuous: a recombination gene, a gamete-size dial starting at 0.50, a post-fusion care dial, a speed dial, and an 8-bit molecular immune lock. Even the renderer has zero if (male) branches — it maps continuous traits straight to bioluminescent color and shape.

Why Negative Controls Sit Right Next to the Experiments

Earlier you caught me falling into smooth textbook dogma. A simulation that always produces sex or always splits into two sexes proves nothing — maybe the deck was stacked. So I built two falsification buttons directly into the top bar:

  • Experiment 3 (Parasites OFF): Clones crush sexual lineages head-to-head because of the real two-fold cost of sex. Turn parasites ON (Experiment 1), and 8-bit Red Queen lock-and-key co-evolution reverses the outcome.
  • Experiment 4 (Benign Ocean, K = 0.015): When tiny+tiny zygotes survive effortlessly, disruptive selection vanishes and the ocean collapses to ~98% uniform small isogamy — two sexes never emerge. Raise K to 0.49 (Experiment 2), and surface-to-volume yolk biophysics tears 0.50 isogamy into a glowing Cyan-and-Amber Y-fork.

What This Model Still Idealizes (Epistemic Honesty)

  • Segregating gamete-size locus: Offspring inherit gameteSize intact from one parent (like a haploid mating-type / sex-determining locus) rather than averaging both parents into a 0.50 hybrid every generation.
  • 2D broadcast spawning & 8-bit immunity: Real marine gamete encounters are 3D turbulent plumes, and real host-parasite immunity involves multi-locus MHC and innate/adaptive cascades rather than a single byte.
  • Care divergence (TEB 3) stays subtle here: Without internal fertilization, gestation, or certainty-of-parentage asymmetry (the placental lock-in we studied earlier), broadcast spawners show only weak care divergence — which itself is a useful scientific clue for Take 03!
